# Neurodivergence (ADHD) and design

Why it matters for NT: distraction, memory load, and long forms hit ADHD hard. Autosave, short steps, and clear progress are required design rules.

## Sources

1. W3C COGA. "Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities."
   - URL: https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/
   - Finding: Objectives include helping users focus, avoiding memory-heavy processes, preventing mistakes, and supporting personalization. Guidance covers cognitive disabilities and neurodiversity.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

2. Nielsen Norman Group. "Minimize Cognitive Load to Maximize Usability."
   - URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/minimize-cognitive-load/
   - Finding: Cutting extraneous load leaves more attention for the real task, which helps people who already struggle to hold focus.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05
