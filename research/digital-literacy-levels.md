# Digital literacy levels

Why it matters for NT: many adults struggle with text and problem solving on screens. Tools must not assume builder vocabulary.

## Sources

1. NCES. "Highlights of the 2023 U.S. PIAAC Results."
   - URL: https://nces.ed.gov/surveys/piaac/2023/national_results.asp
   - Finding: Between 2017 and 2023, the share of U.S. adults ages 16 to 65 at literacy Level 1 or below rose from 19% to 28%; numeracy Level 1 or below rose from 29% to 34%.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

2. W3C. "Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2."
   - URL: https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/
   - Finding: Accessibility guidance includes readable text, predictable operation, and help recovering from mistakes, which support people with lower digital skill.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05
