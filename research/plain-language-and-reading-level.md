# Plain language and reading level

Why it matters for NT: person-facing copy aims for words a 6th grader can read. Banned jargon lives in the rewrite-plan glossary.

## Sources

1. Digital.gov. "Principles of plain language" (adapted from former plainlanguage.gov).
   - URL: https://digital.gov/guides/plain-language/principles
   - Finding: Write for the audience; use active voice, clear structure, and lists; plain language is required for much federal public writing under the Plain Writing Act of 2010.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

2. W3C COGA. "Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities."
   - URL: https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/
   - Finding: Use clear content: easy words, short sentences and blocks, clear images, and easy video.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05
