# Research source rules

## What counts as reputable

Use these first:

- Peer-reviewed papers (journals, conference proceedings with peer review)
- Government sources: NCES, FTC, CFPB, FINRA Foundation, plain language guidance from .gov, W3C (COGA, WCAG)
- Pew Research Center
- Nielsen Norman Group
- OECD

Do not treat as primary proof: blogs without citations, marketing pages, unverified social posts, or model-invented quotes.

## How to cite

For each entry in a topic file:

1. Short title and authors or org
2. URL you opened
3. One-line finding in your own words (no invented figures)
4. Date checked (YYYY-MM-DD)

If you could not open the page, mark it `TODO` and leave the claim out.

## Never invent

Never invent a source, figure, quote, or finding. Prefer a TODO over a guess.
