# Older adults and technology

Why it matters for NT: adults 65+ use the internet far more than a decade ago, but still lag on constant use and some devices. Design for patience, large targets, and plain words.

## Sources

1. Pew Research Center. "Internet use by age" chart.
   - URL: https://www.pewresearch.org/chart/internet-use-by-age-2/
   - Finding: In 2025, about 90% of U.S. adults ages 65+ said they use the internet, up from 75% in 2021 (mode changes apply; treat trend with care).
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

2. Pew Research Center. "Americans' Use of Mobile Technology and Home Broadband" (Jan 31, 2024).
   - URL: https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2024/01/31/americans-use-of-mobile-technology-and-home-broadband/
   - Finding: Adults 65+ are less likely than younger adults to own a smartphone or say they are online almost constantly (15% of 65+ vs 62% of 18-29 in that survey).
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

3. Wild et al. computer self-efficacy and anxiety in older adults (2012). See tech-anxiety-and-self-efficacy.md.
   - URL: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalz.2011.12.008
   - Finding: Older adults' computer confidence and anxiety respond to training and use; design and support matter.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05
