# Attention and cognitive load

Why it matters for NT: every extra word, field, or surprise choice burns limited attention. Person-facing copy targets about grade 6 and short chunks.

## Sources

1. Kathryn Whitenton, Nielsen Norman Group. "Minimize Cognitive Load to Maximize Usability."
   - URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/minimize-cognitive-load/
   - Finding: Extraneous interface work (clutter, unfamiliar patterns, forced recall) uses up working memory and can lead people to abandon the task.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

2. W3C COGA Task Force. "Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities."
   - URL: https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/
   - Finding: Help people focus by cutting distractions, restoring context after interruption, and avoiding processes that depend on memory.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05
