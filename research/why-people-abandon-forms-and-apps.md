# Why people abandon forms and apps

Why it matters for NT: Form Filler and every multi-step tool must cut effort, show progress, and explain errors in plain words.

## Sources

1. Nielsen Norman Group. "Less Effort, More Completion: The EAS Framework for Simplifying Forms."
   - URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/eas-framework-simplify-forms/
   - Finding: More effort makes mid-form abandonment more likely; Eliminate, Automate, then Simplify what remains.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

2. Nielsen Norman Group. "Minimize Cognitive Load to Maximize Usability."
   - URL: https://www.nngroup.com/articles/minimize-cognitive-load/
   - Finding: When mental demand exceeds capacity, people slow down, miss details, or quit.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

3. W3C COGA. "Making Content Usable for People with Cognitive and Learning Disabilities."
   - URL: https://www.w3.org/TR/coga-usable/
   - Finding: Ask only for what you need; make errors easy to fix; support undo and saved progress.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05
