# Financial literacy and money stress (scarcity)

Why it matters for NT: money stress shrinks attention. Money tools must be short, calm, and never invent balances or advice.

## Sources

1. FINRA Investor Education Foundation. "Financial Capability in the United States" (6th Edition, July 2025), National Financial Capability Study.
   - URL: https://www.finrafoundation.org/sites/finrafoundation/files/2025-07/NFCS-Report-Sixth-Edition-July-2025.pdf
   - Finding: Adults with enough emergency savings for three months of expenses fell to 46% from 53% in 2021; more adults report spending above income.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

2. CFPB. "Measuring financial well-being: A guide to using the CFPB Financial Well-Being Scale."
   - URL: https://www.consumerfinance.gov/data-research/research-reports/financial-well-being-scale/
   - Finding: Financial well-being is defined around security and freedom of choice, not income alone, and can be measured with a tested scale.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

3. Harvard IQSS summary of Mullainathan and Shafir, Scarcity.
   - URL: https://www.iq.harvard.edu/news/mullainathan-and-shafir-explore-cognitive-effects-scarcity
   - Finding: Scarcity (including money stress) overloads mental bandwidth and can hurt decision-making and self-control.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05
