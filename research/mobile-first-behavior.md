# Mobile-first behavior

Why it matters for NT: many people, especially lower-income adults, rely on a phone. The website must work at phone size first.

## Sources

1. Pew Research Center. "Americans' Use of Mobile Technology and Home Broadband" (Jan 31, 2024).
   - URL: https://www.pewresearch.org/internet/2024/01/31/americans-use-of-mobile-technology-and-home-broadband/
   - Finding: 90% of U.S. adults had a smartphone in the 2023 survey; 15% were smartphone-dependent (phone but no home broadband), rising to 28% in households under $30,000.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05

2. FINRA Foundation NFCS 6th Edition (July 2025).
   - URL: https://www.finrafoundation.org/sites/finrafoundation/files/2025-07/NFCS-Report-Sixth-Edition-July-2025.pdf
   - Finding: 81% of U.S. adults use a mobile device to access checking or savings accounts.
   - Checked: 2026-11-05
