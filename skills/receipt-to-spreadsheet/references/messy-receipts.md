# Messy receipts

- Faded print: read what you can. Mark the rest "check this". Do not guess a price.
- Crumpled or cut off: list the lines you can see. Tell the person the total may be short.
- Two receipts in one photo: split them. Give each its own store and date.
- Totals printed on the receipt: keep them out of the item rows. Compare them to the script total and report any gap.
- Tips, tax, and discounts: add them as their own rows so the sum matches the paper total.
