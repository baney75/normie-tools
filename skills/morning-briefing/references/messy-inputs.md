# Messy inputs

- Times: capitals, spaces, dots and "at" are fine ("  8:05PM ", "7 a.m.", "at 6 pm"). A time with no am or pm needs a colon and either an hour from 13 to 23 or a leading zero ("07:30"). Anything else is listed under "Time not clear".
- A time with no title: the title is "No title".
- A title with no time: listed under "Time not clear" with just the title.
- Both blank: the row is dropped.
- More than 5 on the day or in reminders: the first 5 are used, and a line says how many were left out.
- Titles over 80 characters are cut at 80.
- Numbers, emoji and odd characters in a title are kept as typed. Control characters become spaces.
- A date that is not real (such as 2026-02-29) is a problem: "Fix this first:". A missing date is fine.
- No part picked is a problem: "Pick at least one part to include."

Read each problem message as it is. Never guess a time or a date.
