# How Save Me Money works

Money is whole cents. Each cost has an amount and how often it is paid.

## Plain-words key

- A year cost: what the cost adds up to in one year.
- A month cost: the year cost split into 12 parts.
- Total per year and total per month: sums of the lines.

## The sums

- Paid every week: year cost = amount times 52. A year is taken as 52 weeks. This is an estimate.
- Paid every month: year cost = amount times 12.
- Paid every year: year cost = the amount.
- Month cost of each line = year cost divided by 12, rounded half up to the cent. This is done once per line.
- Total per year = the sum of the year costs. Total per month = the sum of the month costs.

Because each month cost is rounded, the total per month can be a few cents away from the total per year divided by 12. Example: three costs of $1.00 a year each give month costs of $0.08 each, so the month total is $0.24, while $3.00 divided by 12 is $0.25.

## Order and blanks

Costs are listed biggest year cost first. Equal costs keep the order they were typed. A blank or zero amount is left out.

## Questions

The questions to ask before a call are the same for every cost. They hold no prices, offers or advice.

## Limits

Each amount up to $1,000,000,000. Up to 40 lines. Names keep the first 60 letters.
It does not know prices, offers or contracts. It counts what was typed.
