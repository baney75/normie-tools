# How the Monthly Budget works

Money is whole cents. Every amount is per month.

## Plain-words key

- Income: money that comes in each month.
- Spending: money that goes out each month.
- Left over: income minus spending. Below zero it means more goes out than comes in.
- Percent of income: how much of each income dollar a spending line uses.
- Share of spending: how much of all the spending a line makes up.

## The sums

- Total income = the sum of the income lines.
- Total spending = the sum of the spending lines.
- Left over = total income minus total spending. A negative number is shown with a minus sign.

No rounding happens in these. They are exact to the cent.

## The percents

Percents are whole numbers. They use the largest remainder method so they add up exactly.

1. Each line gets the whole-number part (floor) of 100 times the line divided by the whole.
2. The points still missing are handed out one each to the lines with the biggest leftover fraction.
3. A tie goes to the line higher in the list.

Two sets:

- Percent of income: the whole is total income. They add up to the percent of income that is spent (100 times spending over income, rounded half up). They can add up to more than 100 when spending is more than income.
- Share of spending: the whole is total spending. They add up to exactly 100.

With no income there is no percent of income. With no spending there is no share of spending.

## Limits

Each amount up to $1,000,000,000. Up to 40 lines in each list. Names keep the first 60 letters.
It does not know about taxes, dates, savings goals or what a good budget looks like. It counts what was typed.
